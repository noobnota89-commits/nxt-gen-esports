ABOUT NXT GEN

Terms & Fair Play

Players are expected to play fairly. Hacking, teaming, exploits, abusive behaviour or other unfair play can lead to disqualification. Match-specific rules will be published by the organisers.

Refund & Cancellation

Refunds and slot reopening are handled manually by the organisers. A cancelled/refunded slot may be reopened when confirmed by the organiser.

Privacy

Registration details are used for scrim communication, slot confirmation and tournament administration.
